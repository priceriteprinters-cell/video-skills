# Free Design Resources — No Sign-Up Edition

*Only sources where anyone (including an AI agent) can visit the site and download or use assets immediately, free, with no account, login, email, or payment. Licenses are open/free for commercial use unless noted. Compiled October 2026 from live research.*

**Excluded on purpose:** sites requiring an account (Pixabay downloads, Freesound, ZapSplat, Uppbeat, Sketchfab, TurboSquid, Flaticon, FontSpace, Fontshare, Figma Community, Canva, Videezy, Motion Array, Mixos, Brandfetch), non-commercial-only sites (BBC Sound Effects, StickPNG), and sites with unclear licensing (PNGkey, PNGImages).

**License key:** CC0 = public domain, no attribution. MIT/ISC = open source, keep the license note in code. "Free license" = site's own terms allow commercial use without attribution.

## Quick Picks — safest for anyone, any use (CC0 / MIT, no sign-up)

- **3D/textures:** Poly Haven, ambientCG, Kenney
- **Icons:** Simple Icons, Lucide, Heroicons, Tabler, Phosphor
- **Illustrations:** Open Doodles, ctrlv.design, IRA Design, FreeSVG
- **Images:** PurePNG, Clipart.Free, The Met Open Access, Unsplash, Pexels
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts, Bunny Fonts, Fontsource
- **UI code:** shadcn/ui, HyperUI, daisyUI
- **Sound:** Kenney Audio, Sonniss GDC, Mixkit SFX, Musopen
- **Tools:** Squoosh, SVGOMG, Haikei

## Best for AI agents (direct URLs / APIs, no keys)

Simple Icons CDN, Iconify API, jsDelivr/npm icon packages, Poly Haven API, Google Fonts & Bunny Fonts CSS APIs, Fontsource npm, Kenney ZIPs, Met Open Access API, Unsplash/Pexels direct image links.

*Note: "no sign-up" was verified from site behavior reported in search results; sites can change policies, so a quick check before relying on one in production is wise.*
